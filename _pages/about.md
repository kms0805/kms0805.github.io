Hi! I am a Ph.D. student at the [Machine Intelligence Lab](http://milab.snu.ac.kr) at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. [Kyomin Jung](http://milab.snu.ac.kr/kjung/index.html).

I am broadly interested in **language models** — what they know, how they use what they know, and how we can make them more faithful and trustworthy. My work has centered on **editing and erasing knowledge** inside language models, asking what it takes for a model to genuinely forget or revise a fact rather than merely hide it, and on **how models put knowledge to use**, weighing what they memorized during training against what they are given in context. Recently, I am particularly interested in making **agentic AI systems** easier to **observe** and **control** for humans.
